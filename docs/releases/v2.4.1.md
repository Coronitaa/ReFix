ReFix 2.4.1 fixes LAN play over ZeroTier and VPNs and connections with Steam friends.

### LAN
- **ZeroTier, Radmin, Hamachi and other VPNs:** players on a PC with both a home network and a VPN found each other but could not join the room, because their PC gave out its home network address, which can't be reached over the VPN. ReFix now answers each player at the address their packets actually come from.
- **10.49.x.x home networks:** on a LAN using those addresses, traffic between two instances on the same PC was sent into the Steam tunnel and the second instance timed out. The Steam tunnel now uses a range no real LAN uses.

### Steam friends
- **Connections through Steam:** the Steam helper now handles Steam's networking callbacks like any game, so sessions with friends over the Internet can actually connect. Before, they could stay stuck on "connecting" with nothing getting through.
- **Invite picker:** split into "On the LAN", "Through Steam" and "Steam friends in this game". A friend who plays with ReFix but can't be reached yet shows as connecting, then as not reachable after 15 seconds, instead of "Nobody else on the LAN". The friends card shows the same state.
- **ReFix.log** now records the Steam tunnel's state (relay network, sessions, failed sends and traffic per friend), which helps when a friend can't connect.
