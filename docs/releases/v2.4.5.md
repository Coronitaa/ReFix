ReFix 2.4.5 fixes LAN + Online cross-network invite timeouts, resolves false LAN peer duplicates in the overlay dashboard, and enables instant EOS identity resolution.

### LAN + Online Networking
- **Virtual SteamLink tunnel firewall whitelist:** added the virtual SteamLink tunnel subnet (`198.18.0.0/16`, RFC 2544 benchmark range) to `LanFirewall::IsAllowedIpv4`. This fixes egress packet drops that previously caused `DISCONNECTED Cause: ClientTimeout` in Photon games (R.E.P.O.), `Error al unirse a la sala: La sala no existe` during Unreal/Goldberg lobby discovery (PEAK), and P2P room query timeout in Fusion (Among Us 3D).
- **Overlay UI peer separation:** filtered out virtual tunnel peers (`198.18.x.x`) from the dashboard "Jugadores en la LAN" card and counter (`lanCount`), ensuring remote Steam friends appear exclusively in the "Amigos de Steam" card rather than appearing duplicate under local LAN players.

### EOS SDK & IL2CPP Identity
- **Proactive local identity resolution:** `EOS_Platform_Create` now proactively resolves the local user and registers the ProductUserId and DisplayName with `LobbyDirectory`.
- **Instant HasLocalUser resolution:** `HasLocalUser()` automatically resolves the local user if not already resolved, fixing the empty `EOS ID:` display on title screens (e.g. Among Us 3D) and ensuring immediate authentication readiness when title screen coroutines are bypassed.
