ReFix 2.4.0 brings ReFix Online and ReFix LAN together: LAN play stays free of Steam, and one button adds your Steam friends to the same rooms. It also adds profile sync, a redesigned settings window and a round of multiplayer fixes.

### ReFix Online and ReFix LAN, one ecosystem
- **AutoDeploy has three options** (Windows and Linux):
  - **[1] ReFix Online + LAN** (new default): no Steam client needed, Internet access for the games that need it (`[Network] InternetAccess`), and everything below.
  - **[2] ReFix Offline LAN:** the LAN version of 2.3.2, unchanged, for local network play only.
  - **[3] ReFix Online Legacy:** the Online version of 2.3.2, unchanged.
- **Uninstall** also removes the Photon Fusion Shared server folder (`ReFix\FusionShared`) and the Steam helper files.
- **Steam online services (optional):** with Steam installed, a Steam button in the overlay's top bar connects to Steam as Spacewar, starting Steam in the background (notification area) if it was closed. It asks before it changes anything.
- **Steam friends:** a friends card next to the LAN players, with Steam avatars and status. Friends playing with ReFix come first, the ones in your game at the top, and you can invite them or join their match like LAN players.
- **One room for everyone:** players on your LAN and Steam friends over the Internet can join the same room. The PC with Steam on bridges them.
- **Steam profiles:** every Steam account signed in on the PC becomes a profile with a Steam badge, and the signed-in one is the default while Steam is on.
- **Steam stops showing Spacewar** as soon as you turn it off: the Steam side runs in a small helper process (`refix_steamhost64.exe`) that ReFix ends.

### Profile sync
- **Settings > Syncing:** keep each profile's saves, achievements, statistics and items in one `.rfxp` file.
  - **In the cloud:** sign in to Google Drive or OneDrive. ReFix only gets its own app folder, never your files. Or use the folder of a cloud desktop app.
  - **Local:** a USB drive or any folder, or only this PC.
  - **Export or import:** a `.rfxp` file to carry by hand. Importing always creates a new profile and never replaces one.
- **The sync button** next to your profile shows the state (green tick when synced, orange waiting, red failed) and syncs on click. Your profiles always stay on the PC too, and a newer copy from another PC is taken at game start with the replaced one backed up.
- **Privacy:** the sign-in stays on the PC, encrypted for your Windows user. Nothing is sent to ReFix. Privacy policy: https://coronitaa.github.io/privacy.html

### Overlay
- **Settings window** with a sidebar (Notifications, Sound, Language, Performance, Syncing). The language switch moved there from the top bar.
- **Game icon:** the top bar shows the game's own icon.
- **Several instances:** each instance's overlay stays with its own window and no longer draws over another game or instance.
- **NVIDIA:** with "Turn off NVIDIA Reflex" on (Settings > Performance), Reflex never runs in the game, and NVIDIA App's overlay (its Alt+Z notice, stats and Reflex indicator) stays hidden while the game is in front, so the ReFix overlay no longer flickers.

### Multiplayer fixes
- **Photon rooms over LAN:** fixed joins that timed out (SD-014) when another relay was running in the same game, which swallowed Photon's messages.
- **"Room contains blocked users" (SD-104):** asking the emulator for blocked friends no longer returns every LAN player.
