ReFix 2.3.0 brings Steam achievements to the ReFix LAN overlay, a settings panel, dynamic invite buttons, and a reliable fix for NVIDIA Reflex making the overlay flicker or turn see-through.

### Steam achievements in the overlay
- **Notifications:** when a game unlocks an achievement, a small notice slides in from the corner with its icon, name and rarity, as Steam shows it ("Ultra rare · 2.4% of players"). Progress updates ("3 / 10") get their own notice.
  - The notice stays 5 seconds on screen. Time with the game in the background does not count.
  - Rarer achievements get a reveal of their own: the border is traced in the rarity colour, very rare ones get a light sweep, ultra rare ones a burst of sparkles. Every effect plays once.
  - A soft chime plays with each notice, richer for rarer achievements, and quieter than the game.
- **Achievements card:** the overlay shows how many you have unlocked and your most recent unlocks and progress. "View all" opens the full list with icons, descriptions, unlock dates, progress bars and rarity. Hidden achievements stay hidden until unlocked.
- **Languages:** names and descriptions follow the overlay language (English or Spanish).
- **Per profile:** unlocked achievements, progress and stats are saved in each profile's `stats.ini`, the same file ReFix already used, now with an `[AchievementProgress]` section. Existing data keeps working.
- **Real data for the game:** `GetNumAchievements`, `GetAchievementName`, `GetAchievementDisplayAttribute`, `GetAchievementAchievedPercent`, `GetAchievementProgressLimits`, `IndicateAchievementProgress` and unlock times now answer with the game's real Steam data instead of placeholders.
- **Where the data comes from:** the list comes from Steam's public Web API (no key, no Steam client needed). It is cached in `%APPDATA%\ReFix\Achievements\<AppID>` and refreshed every few days. Icons download once in the background. Offline with no cache, achievements are still recorded and show up once the list is downloaded.
- **Games without Steam achievements** (R.E.P.O., or CS:GO 1.37 under its own AppID) show no achievements card and no notices.

### Settings panel
- A gear in the overlay's top bar opens Settings. Every option is one switch with a plain explanation, and changes are saved at once for every game on the PC (`%APPDATA%\ReFix\profiles.ini`, `[Overlay]`).
  - **Notifications:** unlocked achievements, achievement progress, achievement sounds, invitations (the game still receives them), the welcome notice, and other notices while playing.
  - **Sound and language:** interface sounds and the overlay language.
  - **Performance and compatibility:** pause NVIDIA Reflex while the overlay is open (see below).
  - **Restore defaults** puts every option back.

### NVIDIA Reflex and the overlay
- With Reflex on, the overlay could flicker or turn see-through. While the overlay is open, ReFix now pauses Reflex for the game: low latency off, and `NvAPI_D3D_Sleep` returns at once. When the overlay closes, the game's own Reflex settings come back. The Reflex latency flash square is always dropped.
- The old blocker only took effect when NVAPI was loaded before ReFix, which games rarely do. ReFix now hooks the Reflex functions themselves, retries until the game loads NVAPI, and no longer sets the global variables that switched Reflex, NVAPI under Proton or DXVK-NVAPI off for the whole game.
- On by default. Turn it off in Settings > Performance and compatibility ("Pause NVIDIA Reflex while the overlay is open"), or with `[Overlay] BlockNvidiaReflex = false` in ReFix.ini.

### Invitations
- The invite button follows the player's state:
  - "In the lobby" when they are already in yours.
  - "Sent ✓" for a few seconds after inviting, then "Invite again" while they have not joined.
  - Disabled, with an explanation, when you are not in a lobby.
- The status line under the player reads "Invited · waiting for them to join".
- An invitation notice shows "In the lobby" instead of "Join" when you are already there.

### Look and feel
- Larger minimum text size and bigger captions, so the overlay reads well in small windows.
- Redrawn buttons with clear hover, pressed and disabled states. Disabled buttons have readable labels instead of a faded look.
- The overlay window keeps its own clock for notices. A notice posted mid-frame could close the moment it appeared, and invitations were affected too.

### Fixes
- Achievement icons of newer games download from Steam's current CDN path.
- On networks with broken IPv6, every download waited about 40 seconds before falling back to IPv4. ReFix now falls back at once.
