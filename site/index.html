Play your games together, on your own terms.

ReFix is a free, open-source tool for PC games. It lets players on the same network play together without online servers, adds an in-game overlay, keeps a profile per player with its own saves and achievements, and can connect you with your Steam friends.

Get ReFix on GitHub

LAN multiplayer

Players on the same network find each other and play together, with no server in between.

In-game overlay

Shift+Tab opens players, invites, achievements and settings over the game.

Profiles

Each player has a profile with their own saves, achievements, stats and items.

Steam friends (optional)

Turn it on to see your Steam friends and play with the ones who use ReFix.

Profile sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to, ReFix can keep a copy of your profile file (your saves, achievements, stats and items) in your own Google Drive, so your progress follows you to another PC. This is off until you sign in from the ReFix overlay (Settings > Syncing).

ReFix asks Google only for the drive.appdata permission: a private folder that only ReFix can use. ReFix cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. Your files go directly between your PC and your Google account: they are never sent to ReFix or to anyone else. You can disconnect at any time. Read the privacy policy for the details.
